# IdeaSpace XR – Paper Prototype Test Plan

## 1. Test Purpose

This test explores the core **select–move–place interaction** of IdeaSpace XR, an XR concept inspired by Miro's sticky-note organisation function.

The purpose is not to test the complete IdeaSpace XR system, but to investigate how users naturally attempt to select, move and organise virtual sticky notes in a spatial environment.

---

## 2. Research Question

**Can users intuitively understand how to select, move and place virtual sticky notes when organising information in an XR environment?**

---

## 3. Design Assumption

The test investigates the assumption that users can naturally understand the interaction of:

**Select a sticky note → Move it → Place it into a target category**

without being explicitly instructed which hand gesture or interaction technique they should use.

---

## 4. Paper Prototype

The prototype consists of approximately **12 paper sticky notes** representing virtual sticky notes.

The testing area contains four sections:

* **Unsorted Notes**
* **Problems**
* **Positive Findings**
* **Opportunities**

Participants stand in front of the prototype and imagine that the paper notes are virtual objects within an XR brainstorming environment.

---

## 5. Testing Method

Two methods are used:

### Wizard-of-Oz

The researcher simulates the behaviour of the XR system when necessary, such as confirming when a note has been selected or successfully placed.

### Think-Aloud Protocol

Participants are asked to verbalise what they are thinking while completing the task, including what they expect to happen and any points of confusion.

---

## 6. Participants

The test should involve **at least two participants**.

Participants do not need previous XR experience, as the goal is to observe whether the interaction can be understood naturally.

---

## 7. Participant Task

Participants are given the following instruction:

> Imagine you are in an XR brainstorming environment. Please organise these sticky notes into **Problems, Positive Findings and Opportunities** using whatever interaction feels most natural to you.

Participants are **not told that they should grab, drag or use a specific gesture**, so their natural interaction behaviour can be observed.

---

## 8. Testing Procedure

1. Briefly introduce the IdeaSpace XR scenario without explaining the intended interaction.
2. Show the participant the **Unsorted Notes** area and the three target categories.
3. Ask the participant to organise the sticky notes using whatever interaction feels natural.
4. Ask the participant to think aloud while completing the task.
5. Observe their hand movements, interaction choices and spatial behaviour.
6. Only provide assistance if the participant is unable to continue.
7. After the task, conduct a short follow-up discussion about:

   * what felt natural;
   * what felt confusing;
   * how they expected a selected note to respond;
   * how they expected the system to confirm successful placement.

---

## 9. Data to Collect

During each session, record:

* Task completion time
* Whether the first attempted interaction is successful
* Number of times assistance is required
* Number of placement or classification errors
* Interaction or gestures attempted
* Whether the participant uses one or two hands
* Any large body movement or reaching required
* Key comments from the Think-Aloud session
* Participant confusion or suggestions

Photos or notes from the prototype testing should also be retained as supporting evidence.

---

## 10. Analysis Focus

The results will be organised into three areas:

### Interaction

How users naturally attempt to select, move and release sticky notes.

### Spatial Layout

Whether the location and distance of the notes and category areas are comfortable and understandable.

### System Feedback

What visual, audio or physical feedback users expect when selecting and placing a note.

The findings will be used to inform the next digital XR prototype and determine what interaction feedback should be implemented.
